Table rules

Bet at least 500 coins on Player, Banker or Tie. Highest last digit wins: aces count as 1; tens and face cards count as 0. An opening 8 or 9 is a natural and stops all drawing.

Player draws on 0–5 and stands on 6–7. If Player stands, Banker draws on 0–5. Otherwise Banker draws on 0–2; on 3 except against an 8; on 4 against 2–7; on 5 against 4–7; and on 6 against 6–7. Banker stands on 7.

Player wins pay 1:1. Banker wins pay 0.95:1 after 5% commission. Tie bets pay 8:1 if the hands tie and lose otherwise. Player and Banker bets return their stake on a tie. When betting Tie, choose which hand to peel. Revealing a card does not change the result. Balances below 500 refill to 5,000 after a completed hand.

Drag upward from a card to squeeze it. Release before the threshold to put it back, or use “Reveal my cards.” Keyboard: focus a card and press Enter or Space.
